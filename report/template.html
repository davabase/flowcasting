Forecast against ground truth

Four test cases from the summer and autumn of 2019, months the model never saw in training. Each animation plays the 13 observed frames, then the 12 forecast frames side by side with what really happened. The three strongest storms were picked by how much heavy precipitation the radar recorded, not by which method scored best; the fourth is a random moderate case.

Encoded vertically integrated liquid (VIL, 0–255). Tick marks are the six intensity thresholds used for scoring: 16 is light rain, 133 a heavy storm core, 219 the most extreme cells.

Scorecard

CSI-M, mean critical success index over six VIL thresholds

MSE is on the 0–1 scaled VIL (×10⁻³). BIAS-133 above 1 means the method draws too much heavy rain, below 1 too little. Pool4 and pool16 compute CSI after 4×4 and 16×16 km max-pooling, which forgives small position errors.
